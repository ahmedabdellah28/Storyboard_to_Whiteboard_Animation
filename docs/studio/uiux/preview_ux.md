# Whiteboard Studio — Preview UX Guidelines

> **Experience Principle:** Live Preview Without Interruption  

---

## 1. Zero-Friction Playback
- Transport controls (`Play`, `Pause`, `Stop`, `Scrub`) reside directly within the Studio toolbar.
- The user does not need to export, compile, or wait for background FFmpeg processes to preview visual pacing and audio alignment.

## 2. Playhead Scrubbing
- Dragging the playhead provides immediate real-time feedback:
  - Elements update their visibility and inking progress instantaneously.
  - Camera frustum reframes to the correct shot.
  - Timecode display reports exact minutes, seconds, and milliseconds (`00:04.20 / 00:39.45`).

## 3. Scope Selection
- Quick toggle buttons (`Shot`, `Section`, `Selection`, `Project`) allow zooming the playback focus without switching screens.
